Health Insurance Frequently Asked Questions

Is your consultation really free?

Yes. The consultation is meant to help you understand your options and decide whether there is a plan that fits your situation.

What insurance companies do you work with?

I help clients compare options available to them based on their needs, location, eligibility, and preferences.

Can you help self-employed individuals?

Yes. Many clients I help are self-employed, contractors, business owners, or people who do not have access to employer coverage.

Do you only work with clients in Tennessee?

No. Hinesight Solutions is based in Chattanooga, Tennessee, and James serves clients in 20 states where he is licensed.

How long does the process take?

Most first conversations are quick and focused. The goal is to understand your needs and show you the clearest path forward.

Can I keep my current doctor?

That depends on the plan and network. During your review, we can talk through doctors, prescriptions, and any must-have benefits.

Can you help me compare private or PPO health insurance options?

Yes. When private health insurance or PPO network options are available for your location and eligibility, we can review how those options compare with other coverage paths.

What if I need help after I enroll?

You can reach out after enrollment for help understanding your policy, cards, next steps, or general questions.
