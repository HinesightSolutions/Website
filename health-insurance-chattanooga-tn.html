A local advisor who understands the questions

Chattanooga residents can face very different coverage needs depending on employment, household income, age, health history, and preferred healthcare systems. Someone working independently downtown may prioritize nationwide network access, while a family in Hixson or Ooltewah may focus on pediatric care, prescriptions, and predictable out-of-pocket costs.

James helps residents of Chattanooga, Hixson, East Ridge, Red Bank, Ooltewah, Soddy-Daisy, and surrounding Hamilton County communities organize those priorities before comparing options.

Coverage for local doctors and care

A provider logo or network name is only the beginning. Before enrolling, confirm how the specific plan treats your doctors, hospitals, urgent care centers, specialists, and prescriptions. Network participation can vary by plan and can change over time.

James helps review available directories and plan information, then encourages final confirmation with the carrier and provider when access to a particular doctor or facility is essential.

Help for employees, contractors, and business owners

Chattanooga’s workforce includes traditional employees, independent contractors, healthcare workers, tradespeople, commission-based professionals, entrepreneurs, and small business owners. If employer coverage is unavailable, unaffordable, or ending, James can help compare Marketplace and private routes based on eligibility and coverage needs.

Appointments are available by phone or online, so you can get help without driving across town or rearranging a workday.
