Your income and healthcare needs both matter

Self-employed income can fluctuate, which makes the balance between monthly premiums and potential out-of-pocket costs especially important. A low premium can leave you exposed to a large deductible, while richer benefits may cost more each month than you realistically want to carry.

James helps contractors, consultants, commission-based professionals, gig workers, and business owners compare the total picture instead of focusing on one number.

Questions worth answering before you apply

  • Do you qualify for Marketplace premium assistance based on household income?
  • Do you need coverage for pre-existing conditions, maternity, mental health, or recurring prescriptions?
  • Which doctors, hospitals, and provider networks matter most?
  • How much financial exposure could your business and household absorb?
  • Do you travel or work across state lines?

Your answers help determine whether an ACA Marketplace plan, a private option, or another route deserves closer attention.

Coverage for owners, spouses, and families

Being self-employed does not mean you must evaluate coverage in isolation. James can help review needs for a spouse, children, or business partner and explain whether it makes sense to compare the household together or consider different solutions. If you are evaluating a tax deduction or business reimbursement arrangement, coordinate with a qualified tax professional because tax treatment depends on your structure and circumstances.

Common questions

Questions to ask before choosing coverage

Can self-employed people use the Marketplace?

Yes. Self-employed individuals can apply through the Marketplace, and eligibility for premium tax credits depends on household information and income.

Are private plans available year-round?

Some private options may accept applications outside annual Open Enrollment, but availability, medical underwriting, benefits, and eligibility vary.

Can health insurance be tax deductible?

Some self-employed individuals may qualify for a health insurance deduction. James does not provide tax advice, so confirm your eligibility with a qualified tax professional.
