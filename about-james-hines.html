A personal approach to a complicated decision

Health insurance decisions affect far more than a monthly premium. A plan can look attractive until you discover that a doctor is outside the network, a prescription is handled differently than expected, or the deductible does not work the way you assumed. James helps clients slow the decision down just enough to understand those details before enrolling.

Appointments are conversational and focused. You can explain what is not working, what coverage you have had before, and what matters most now. James then helps organize the available information so you can compare options with more confidence.

Who James works with

James regularly helps individuals and families without employer-sponsored coverage, self-employed professionals, independent contractors, entrepreneurs, and small business owners. Hinesight Solutions is based in Chattanooga and serves clients virtually in 20 states where James is licensed.

  • Individuals changing jobs or leaving employer coverage
  • Families comparing premiums, networks, and out-of-pocket exposure
  • Self-employed professionals building coverage around variable income
  • Business owners evaluating options for themselves or their teams
  • Clients who want help understanding policy details after enrollment

What to expect when you talk

The first conversation is designed to identify your priorities—not pressure you into a decision. Be ready to discuss who needs coverage, preferred doctors and hospitals, prescriptions, expected care, current coverage, and a comfortable monthly range. When a particular option is not a fit, James will say so and explain why.

After enrollment, James remains available to help with general policy questions, cards, next steps, and understanding where to direct carrier-specific service needs.
